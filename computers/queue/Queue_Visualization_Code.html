Queue Rules (FIFO)

  • Remove takes the FRONT item.
  • Insert adds an item at the BACK.
  • Drag the front item inside the queue to move it to the back.
  • Drag the front item outside the queue to remove it.
  • Press Enter on the selected front item to move it to the back.
  • Sentinels: -1 (numbers), '-' (char), highlighted in red.
  • Up to 10 items. Integers 0–99; doubles 0 to less than 100; chars A–Z or a–z.
