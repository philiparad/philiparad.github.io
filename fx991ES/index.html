Natural Textbook Display

Use ◀ ▶ to navigate expressions and the upper/lower slots; ▲ ▼ switch numerator and denominator. Press ) to exit the current template. Empty slots must be completed before calculating. The linear source field remains available for teaching input.

Numeric-format example: Rnd(1/3)*3 with Fix 2 gives 0.99, because Rnd changes the stored result of its argument. Formatting examples: 2.675 in Fix 2 → 2.68; 1/200 in Norm 1 → 5e-3, Norm 2 → 0.005. Implicit multiplication: 2(3+4) → 14, 2pi → 2π, 1/2pi → 1/(2π). Other COMP examples: -2^2, sqrt(8), 1/3+1/6, sin(30). CMPLX prototype: 2,3;4,5 (addition). BASE-N: BIN:1010 AND 1100.
