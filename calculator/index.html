Modes and setup

Everything is entered through the calculator. MODE opens the LCD mode menu; press 1–6 to choose an item, ◀/▶ for pages. In multi-field screens, enter a value, press = to advance, and AC to exit. SHIFT → MODE opens LCD setup.

Press MODE, then a numbered key (1–6). Use ▶ for the next menu page and ◀ to return. SHIFT + MODE opens SETUP. AC exits. Use only calculator buttons for calculations.
